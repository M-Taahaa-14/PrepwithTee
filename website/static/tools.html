Tools that know
your syllabus.

Not generic calculators and copy-pasted formula lists. Every tool here is built from the official Cambridge syllabus for your exact course code — so it knows which papers ban the calculator and which formulas you are expected to have memorised.

Every one of these also opens inside the past-paper builder, progress tracker and the AI tutor — look for the Tools button in the bottom corner, or press Alt + T. You never have to leave a question to look something up.

🧮

Scientific calculator

Full scientific keypad with DEG/RAD, memory and a running history. Tell it your subject and it warns you when the paper you are practising is non-calculator — then locks itself if you want to train properly.

Live 📐

Formula sheets

169 formulas across Maths, Physics and Chemistry, each marked Given in exam or Must memorise. IGCSE Physics provides nothing at all — this shows you exactly what that means.

Live 📈

Graph plotter

Plot quadratics, cubics, reciprocals and trig curves. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom, hover to trace — and it reads off the roots and turning points in view.

Live 📐

Graph sketching guide

Master graph shapes, asymptotes, and coordinate calculations. Includes step-by-step notes on sketching quadratic and cubic curves, and finding cubic equations from given graphs. For O Level, IGCSE & A Levels.

New 💡

Command words

State, Describe, Explain, Suggest — Cambridge's own definitions for your syllabus, what each one is really asking for, and real past-paper questions using them.

Live ⚗️

Periodic table

The Cambridge table with the exact relative atomic masses printed in your exam paper, plus a mole calculator that parses formulas like Ca(OH)₂ and CuSO₄.5H₂O.

Live 🔢

Number bases & logic

Denary, binary, hex and octal with the place-value working shown, two’s complement step by step, and truth tables with every intermediate column — where the marks actually are.

Live ⌨️

Pseudocode runner

Write Cambridge pseudocode and run it. Errors come with the line number and a suggested fix, it flags Python habits creeping in, and it builds the trace table for you.

Live 🧮

Grade calculator

Enter your paper marks and predict your overall grade using official Cambridge grade thresholds and component weightings.

New 📈

Grade thresholds history

Visualise how Cambridge grade thresholds and boundaries have changed over the last 10 years to spot syllabus trends.
