Common questions

Can I try before paying?

Yes — the Free plan is free forever with no card required. You get the dashboard, yearly past papers, study tools and 10 AI Tutor queries a month. Upgrade only when you're ready.

What counts as a "subject"?

Each Cambridge syllabus code is one subject — e.g. O Level Maths (4024), IGCSE Physics (0625). Solo lets you pick one; 3 Subjects lets you pick any three from our library.

What are the session packages (Oct/Nov, May/Jun)?

One-time lump-sum payments that give you full access until the exam session closes. Great value if you're sitting a specific series — you pay once and revise as much as you need until exam day.

Can I change my subject mid-subscription?

Yes, you can change your active subject from your profile page. The change takes effect immediately.

How big are the group classes?

Batches are capped at 6 students — small enough that Sir Taahaa still marks your work and answers your questions in the lesson, but shared enough to bring the fee down to roughly half the one-on-one rate. Seats go first come, first served, so batches fill quickly before an exam session.

Can I still get 1-on-1 tutoring with Tee?

Yes — switch the tutoring plans above to One-on-one to see those rates. You get private lessons at times you choose. Either way, message us on WhatsApp to book a free demo lesson; there's no commitment until after you've met the tutor.

Is there a cancellation fee?

No. Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time and you retain access until the end of the billing period. Session packages are one-time purchases and are non-refundable after activation.
