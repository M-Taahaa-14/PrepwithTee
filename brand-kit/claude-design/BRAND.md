# PrepWithTee - brand guide (for social posts)

prepwithtee.com - Cambridge O Level / IGCSE / A Level tutoring from Lahore, run by
"Tee". Topical past papers, mock tests, MCQ practice, notes and free AI help for
Maths (4024, 0580, 9709), Physics (5054, 0625, 9702), Computer Science (2210, 0478)
and more.

Tagline: **"You already work hard. Let's make it count."**

## Type (Google Fonts)

- Headlines: **Playfair Display** 700-900 (serif, italic 600 for emphasis words)
- Numbers, labels, stats, small caps-style tags: **Archivo** 700-800
- Body text: **Hanken Grotesk** 400-600

## Shape and style

- Rounded corners: 18px cards, 26px large panels, pill buttons/chips.
- Light, warm, colourful - cream backgrounds with pastel tiles, never
  near-black. Dark variants use deep purple #2E1B4A, not black.
- Gold #E8913A for key numbers and thin accent rules.
- Mascot: the owl (logos/owl-icon-*.png). Use the full logo or the owl, never
  stretch or recolour them. White versions on purple backgrounds.

## Voice

Warm, encouraging, direct; talks to students (14-18) and their parents.
Short sentences, exam-specific ("Paper 2", "mark scheme", "A*"), no hype.
Calls to action: "Practise this chapter", "Book a free demo", WhatsApp contact.
