Questions parents ask

Can we try before paying anything?

Yes. The first lesson is a free demo — you meet the tutor, see the past-paper material and watch how a lesson actually runs. No card details, no obligation.

What if we need to stop mid-term?

Billing is monthly, so you stop at the end of the month you've paid for. There's no annual contract and no cancellation fee.

Are the past papers included, or extra?

Included, entirely. The topical builder, the mark schemes, the topic tests and the full paper library all come with any plan.

Which boards and subjects are covered?

Cambridge O Level, IGCSE and A Level — Mathematics, Physics and Computer Science. Every course code is listed on the subjects page.

Do lessons happen online or in person?

Online one-on-one, so there's no commute and lessons can be recorded for revision. Message us on WhatsApp if you'd like to discuss in-person options.
