How we teach · what you get

See exactly how a week works.

These are real screens from PrepWithTee, the platform every Physics student uses alongside the live classes. It's included in the fee, with nothing extra to buy.

In class

Taught live, on a shared board

Every class is live with the tutor in a small group. Real exam diagrams go up on the PrepWithTee Board, where we draw, annotate and work through them together with a ruler, protractor, compass and graph paper. Nobody hides at the back of a group of six.

  • Concept first, then straight into exam questions
  • Every student answers, every answer gets checked
  • Board notes saved for revision

Homework

Topical past papers on what was just taught

After each chapter, students get a booklet of real Cambridge questions on exactly that chapter, from every recent session and variant. Original papers, not retyped, so diagrams and answer lines look just like the exam.

  • Every recent question on the chapter, newest first
  • Official mark scheme straight after each question
  • Annotate on screen or print it

Stuck at 11 pm?

Mark schemes, explained step by step

Next to every question is the official mark scheme and a worked explanation that shows where each mark comes from, plus hints when a student only wants a nudge. Anything still unclear goes to the tutor on WhatsApp.

  • See why an answer gets the marks
  • Hints before the full solution
  • WhatsApp the tutor for anything else

Day 5, every week

A weekly test built from past papers

Each Day 5 test is built from past-paper questions on that week's chapters: multiple choice first, then structured questions, with its own mark scheme. It's timed like the real exam.

  • Covers exactly what was taught that week
  • Timed, then marked to the official scheme
  • Scores shared with parents

Results

Marks and a grade, not just a tick

Students record their marks against the official grade thresholds for that exact paper, so everyone can see the grade a score would have earned, and how close the next grade is.

  • Real grade thresholds for every paper
  • Progress across past papers
  • Know what A* actually takes

Ask on WhatsApp
How does the money-back guarantee work?
If you're not satisfied after your first paid class, tell us and we refund that month's fee in full. You can also sit a free demo class before paying anything.
What does a week look like?
Four classes, then a test on the fifth day. It's the same every week from November to April, and test scores are shared with parents.
Why finish the syllabus by January?
So February to April can go entirely on past paper sessions: full papers under timed conditions, marked to the official mark scheme, with weak topics re-taught before the exam.
Are AS and A2 Physics separate courses?
Yes. AS (Papers 1, 2 and 3) and A2 (Papers 4 and 5) each have their own batch and timetable, so you only join the part you're sitting. Taking both? Ask us about bundle fees.
Are the classes online or in person?
Both. Classes run live online, and in person in Lahore, in groups of four to six students.
Can I join after 31 October?
Yes, while seats are left. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll help you catch up on the topics already covered.
What else is included?
Full access to the PrepWithTee platform: topical past papers with mark schemes, the test builder, MCQ practice and AI help, so homework and revision happen in one place.
