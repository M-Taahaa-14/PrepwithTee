Got questions?

Parents usually ask…

How do lessons actually work?

One tutor, one student — online or in person. Each lesson follows a plan built from the official Cambridge syllabus for your child's exact course code, and ends with a custom topical worksheet as homework.

Which subjects and boards do you cover?

Cambridge O Level (Mathematics D 4024, Physics 5054, Computer Science 2210), IGCSE (Mathematics 0580, Physics 0625, Computer Science 0478), and A Level on enquiry. Every course is taught against its own syllabus — no mixing.

Where do the practice materials come from?

Real Cambridge past papers, 2020–2025, every session and variant. Our engine slices them by topic so practice always matches what was just taught — with the official mark scheme attached to every question.

How do I see my child's progress?

After every topic test you get a chapter-by-chapter breakdown scored against the real mark scheme — which topics are secure, which get retaught. No vague "doing well", ever.

Is the first lesson really free?

Yes — a full demo lesson with no commitment. See the teaching and the materials, then decide.

What are the fees?

PKR 20,000 a month for one subject, PKR 35,000 for two and PKR 50,000 for three — billed monthly, no joining fee and no annual contract. Every plan includes the topical past papers, mark schemes, topic tests and the full paper library. See the full breakdown.
