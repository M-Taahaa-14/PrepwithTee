Which subjects and syllabi do you cover?
Cambridge O Level (Maths 4024, Physics 5054, Chemistry 5070, Computer Science 2210, Islamiyat 2058, Pakistan Studies 2059), IGCSE (Maths 0580, Physics 0625, Chemistry 0620, Computer Science 0478) and A Level (Maths 9709, Physics 9702, Computer Science 9618). Everything is aligned to the current Cambridge syllabi. Not sure which syllabus your school uses? Tell us your school name and we'll confirm.
Are sessions online or in-person?
All sessions run online via Zoom or Google Meet, so students anywhere in the world can join. Our platform and past-paper resources are fully web-based — no app to install. If you're in Lahore and prefer in-person, get in touch and we'll discuss what's possible.
How does the topical past-paper system work?
We've broken every past paper from 2020–2025 into individual questions, classified each one by syllabus topic, and built a system that generates custom practice sets for any topic you choose. You get the original exam crop — diagrams, graphs, and all — plus the mark scheme alongside. It's the fastest way to practise exactly what you struggle with.
Can I try the platform before enrolling?
Yes — study notes, formula sheets, the AI Tutor, and several other tools are available to browse without an account. Send us a message here and we'll arrange a free introductory session so you can see how we teach before committing.
How much does tutoring cost?
Pricing depends on the subject, session frequency, and whether you're enrolling solo or in a small group. Visit the Pricing page for current plans, or send us a message and we'll put together the right package for your situation.
How quickly will you reply to my message?
We aim to reply within 24 hours on weekdays. Messages sent over the weekend are answered first thing Monday. For urgent matters, email nexgentutors6@gmail.com directly.
I'm a teacher — how do I apply?
We work with qualified Cambridge teachers who want to reach students globally. Head to the Teach with Us page to submit an application. We review every application personally and reply within a week.
My child missed a session — is there a recording?
Yes, all live sessions are recorded with the student's consent and shared privately via a secure link for 30 days after the session. Let us know in your message if recordings are important to you and we'll confirm the arrangement upfront.
